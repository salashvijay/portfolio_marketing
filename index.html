Invoice to e-invoice

Belgium already requires structured e-invoices between businesses. Germany follows in 2027 and 2028, French small businesses from 1 September 2027, and the UAE from July 2027. Most businesses still receive PDF and paper invoices from suppliers.

  1. ReadUpload a PDF, scan, or photo of an invoice.
  2. ConvertGet structured EN 16931 data: UBL, Factur-X, or XRechnung.
  3. CheckA validator shows exactly what is missing, and flags the same invoice arriving twice.

In development. We do not replace certified platforms or Peppol access points; we prepare clean data for them. Accounting firms and software vendors who want early access: write to us with your country and monthly invoice volume.
