What this checks

  • The mandatory EN 16931 core fields: specification ID, invoice number, dates, type, currency, seller and buyer names and countries, and on each line the item, quantity, price and VAT category.
  • The totals: line amounts, net total, VAT total, gross total and amount due must add up (rules BR-CO-10, 13, 15 and 16).
  • The common Peppol and XRechnung extras: electronic addresses and the buyer reference.

This is a fast first check, not the full official Schematron validation that certified platforms and access points run. A passing result means the basics are right, not that the invoice is certified. CII (Factur-X/ZUGFeRD) support is coming.
