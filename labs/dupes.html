Duplicate invoice finder

The same supplier invoice entered twice is one of the commonest ways a business pays twice. Paste a CSV export from your accounts or payables list and see which invoices look like duplicates.

🔒 Your list is checked in this page only. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Open a .csv file: or paste below.

What it looks for

  1. Same supplier, invoice number and amount. Upper and lower case, spaces, dashes, leading zeros and endings like "Ltd" or "Pvt" are ignored, so INV-0042 and inv 42 match.
  2. Same supplier and invoice number, different amount or date. A number should be used once; this can be a re-issued or corrected invoice, or a typing error.
  3. Same supplier, amount and date, different invoice number. Often the same invoice re-keyed with a changed number (for example a suffix added).

What it can't tell you

  • A flagged group is a possible duplicate. Suppliers do bill the same amount on the same day, and credit notes or part-payments can repeat a number. A person should check the original documents.
  • It reads the columns by their header names (Supplier or Vendor, Invoice number or Invoice no, Date or Invoice date, Amount or Total). Commas, semicolons, tabs and pipes are all accepted. Dates are read day-first (31/10/2026) or as 2026-10-31.
  • It only compares rows inside the list you give it, not against payments made earlier or in another system.
