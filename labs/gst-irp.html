India GST e-invoice: 30-day IRP deadline calculator

If your annual turnover is Rs 10 crore or more, an e-invoice, credit note or debit note older than 30 days can no longer be reported to the IRP. Enter the document date to see how long you have.

🔒 Nothing is sent anywhere. The dates are worked out in your browser.

What this checks, and what it doesn't

  • It counts 30 calendar days from the document date, as in the GSTN advisory of 5 November 2024 (effective 1 April 2025, lowering the limit from Rs 100 crore to Rs 10 crore turnover).
  • It covers invoices, credit notes and debit notes that need an IRN. Below Rs 10 crore the advisory says there is no such limit "for now".
  • It does not know whether your business must issue e-invoices at all, which turnover figure counts for you, or whether the portal has changed the rule since. Check the current GSTN notices.
  • The advisory does not make clear whether day 30 itself is allowed, so the tool suggests reporting 3 days earlier.

Source: GSTN advisory, "Time Limit for Reporting e-Invoice on the IRP Portal: Lowering of Threshold to AATO 10 Crores and Above" (copy on the Maharashtra GST site). Information, not legal or tax advice.
